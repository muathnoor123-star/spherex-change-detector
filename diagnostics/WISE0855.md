# Why the detector missed WISE 0855

Across the 29 eligible exposures, 21 missed the image proposal threshold and eight produced a nearby peak that the forest scored below the review threshold. Every eligible exposure had three reference images. The blind scan returned zero matching motion alerts, reproducing the original evaluation.

| Outcome within two pixels of the catalogue position | Frames |
|---|---:|
| Largest valid absolute difference below 3.5 noise units | 21 |
| Peak proposed, then rejected by review score | 8 |
| Accepted alert | 0 |

The median aperture signal-to-noise ratio was 9.15. That measurement describes the source flux in one exposure. The detector looks for a change after subtracting a reference. The median strongest difference within the match radius was only 2.37 noise units. Across the eight matching proposals, review scores ranged from 0.010 to 0.055 against a threshold of 0.592. A spatial match alone does not establish that WISE 0855 caused the residual.

The catalogue gives a total apparent displacement of 1.35 pixels across the stack. For each eligible frame we calculated its predicted distance from the target in each of its three references. The median of the 29 per-frame medians was 0.77 pixels. These small offsets are consistent with partial cancellation during subtraction. This measurement does not isolate the effects of wavelength, noise or nearby sources.

A follow-up experiment could combine evidence across epochs along candidate motion paths and compare against static controls. Select parameters on training and validation fields before testing on fresh sky regions. This target has now been inspected and should be treated as a diagnostic example in subsequent development.

Run `python diagnose_wise0855.py path/to/planetx-dataset-v1.2.0` from the repository root with the saved model extracted. The [per-frame measurements](wise0855_frames.csv) list each eligible frame, its residual evidence and the first stage that rejected it. Catalogue positions are used only to evaluate the blind proposals.
